# Terms

Every studio must accept the **Developer terms** before creating games and uploading versions. The full text, always the latest version, lives on Game Hub:

**[play.monlabs.fun/terms/developer](https://play.monlabs.fun/terms/developer)**

The terms are versioned. When a new version comes out, the studio owner needs to click **I agree** on the Developer page before the studio can create games and upload again – published games keep running.

Key points to keep in mind when preparing your game:

- You **keep ownership** of your game; Game Hub only gets the right to host and publish it, and to use its name, cover and description to promote it
- You must hold the rights to everything in the game (code, images, audio, fonts, names)
- No gambling or real-money mechanics, content that breaks Vietnamese law, malware, crypto mining, or showing your own ads or taking payments outside Game Hub
- Don't try to escape the hub's isolated frame ([runtime environment](/en/moi-truong)) or collect player data
- Only submit real scores through the SDK – scores showing signs of cheating are removed ([anti-cheat](/en/bang-xep-hang))
- There's **no revenue share program yet**; if one comes, it will have its own terms
- To take your game down: ask an admin any time

This page is only a summary; where they differ, the version on Game Hub is the one in force.
